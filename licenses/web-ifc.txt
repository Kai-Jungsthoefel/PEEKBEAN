Third-party components compiled into web-ifc.wasm
==============================================================================

web-ifc (MPL-2.0, https://github.com/ThatOpen/engine_web-ifc) compiles these header-only C++ libraries into
its WebAssembly module, as declared in its src/cpp/CMakeLists.txt. All of them are compatible with
GPL-3.0-or-later.

- fast_float: MIT (also available under Apache-2.0 or BSL-1.0)
  https://github.com/fastfloat/fast_float
- tinynurbs: BSD-3-Clause
  https://github.com/QuimMoya/tinynurbs (fork of https://github.com/pradeep-pyro/tinynurbs)
- GLM (OpenGL Mathematics): MIT (or the Happy Bunny License)
  https://github.com/g-truc/glm
- earcut.hpp: ISC
  https://github.com/mapbox/earcut.hpp
- CDT (constrained Delaunay triangulation): MPL-2.0
  https://github.com/artem-ogre/CDT
- spdlog: MIT
  https://github.com/gabime/spdlog
- {fmt} (bundled with spdlog): MIT
  https://github.com/fmtlib/fmt
- stduuid (Marius Bancila): MIT
  https://github.com/mariusbancila/stduuid
- unordered_dense: MIT
  https://github.com/martinus/unordered_dense
